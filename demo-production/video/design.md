# Design direction

**Source:** toolkit video-design (`ours`), adapted from the incident report's
graphite, green, and coral palette.

**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps. This 76-second highlight is a historical
GitHub/incident-report excerpt, not the full live collaboration demo.

- Dark graphite is the base field. Warm off-white carries readable evidence.
- Leaf green marks a verified fix; coral marks an invalid or blocked report.
- Menlo for revisions and field names; Arial for narration-led headings.
- Motion is restrained: one reveal per scene, then a still reading hold.
- The browser footage is a real capture of the incident report and public GitHub
  PR, labeled with capture and historical dates.
- No invented dashboard, live task metrics, mock agent messages, or fictitious
  test logs appear.
- The full Switch / Discord / two-user workflow remains a separate, blocked
  recording task. This excerpt must not imply that it was recorded.
