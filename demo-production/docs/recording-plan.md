## Capture rules

- Keep the raw captures unmodified and make edit copies.
- Keep the two users' identities tied to their own authenticated sessions.
- Label every historical segment with its actual date and PR/task reference.
- Do not recreate messages, handoffs, receipts, terminal output, or PR UI.
- Do not capture unrelated server lists, DMs, credentials, or runtime secrets.
- Do not state exact final timestamps until the final output has been rendered and
  checked with `ffprobe`.
